# Agent 1 — Research Agent

## Purpose
Gather market context, company background, and role-specific intelligence to ground all outputs in real data.

## Prompt used

```
You are a senior product research analyst. I have an interview for a Supply Product Manager role at FYUL — the company formed by the merger of Printify, Printful, and Snow Commerce.

The interviewer is Marcel Kilgenstein, Director of Product for the Supply Tech pillar.

Please research and synthesize:
1. FYUL's business model, the Supply Tech pillar's mission, and the Decorator ecosystem
2. The cultural and communication style expected (based on the job description)
3. Key technical concepts relevant to the role: self-onboarding, catalog data pipelines, AI/LLM applications in supply chain, print-on-demand technology
4. The premium leather goods market opportunity in print-on-demand

Output a structured briefing document in Markdown. Use specific data points, not vague claims. Flag any assumptions you make.
```
