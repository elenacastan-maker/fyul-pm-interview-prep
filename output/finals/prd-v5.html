Field What it classifies Value captured Buyer segment unlocked Price impact
leather_grade full-grain / top-grain / bonded / PU The single most critical FTC compliance field. Without it, "full-grain" and "bonded" are indistinguishable in listings — legal liability for Printify and decorators. Also the #1 filter buyers use in premium leather search. All premium leather buyers. Required for marketplace category ranking algorithms on Etsy and Amazon. Full-grain commands 40–60% price premium over bonded at equivalent SKU
tanning_method vegetable / chrome / aldehyde / combination "Vegetable-tanned" is the highest-volume premium search term on Etsy for leather goods. Unlocks organic search traffic from buyers with high purchase intent. Also a gating requirement in several EU markets (chrome VI restrictions). Eco-conscious buyers, craft leather enthusiasts, EU market. Etsy top search: "vegetable tanned leather wallet". Vegetable-tanned commands 20–35% premium vs chrome-tanned equivalent
finish_type aniline / semi-aniline / pigmented / nubuck / suede Aniline leather shows natural grain and develops patina over time — the hallmark of luxury leather goods. Pigmented is more durable but lower perceived value. This field directly determines price ceiling and buyer expectation alignment. Luxury and craft buyers who understand leather quality. Aniline buyers have highest LTV and lowest return rate. Aniline SKUs command 2–3× the price of pigmented equivalents
hide_thickness_mm 0.6 – 3.0 mm numeric Enables technical filtering by application (wallet = 0.8–1.2mm / bag = 1.4–2.0mm). Professional and B2B buyers require this. Consistent thickness is also a quality signal — premium tanneries hold tight tolerances; commodity ones don't. B2B buyers, custom makers, professional leather workers. Enables Printify to serve wholesale channels. Correct thickness match reduces returns by an estimated 15–20% — direct margin protection
hide_origin Country of hide (Italy / Spain / Argentina / USA) "Italian leather" is a globally recognised quality signal with no regulation — but buyers pay for it. Origin drives premium pricing perception and enables marketplace "Made with Italian leather" filter badges. Argentine hides are known for softness; Spanish for durability. Premium fashion buyers, gift market, corporate gifting. Origin is the #1 stated purchase criterion for premium leather gifts. Italian-origin listing: 20–35% higher ASP vs unspecified origin at same grade
grain_correction natural grain / corrected grain Natural grain = highest tier (no buffing to hide imperfections — every hide is unique). Corrected grain = uniform surface, still quality leather but not top-tier. Prevents misrepresentation (FTC concern) and helps premium buyers identify genuine quality. Direct correlate of price ceiling. Discerning luxury buyers who research before buying. Buyers who have returned "misrepresented" leather before. Natural grain sets the price ceiling — corrected grain caps at ~60–70% of natural grain ASP
compliance_cert LWG Gold·Silver·Bronze / REACH / Chrome VI-free / OEKO-TEX LWG certification is the B2B industry standard — retailers like H&M and Nike require it from suppliers. REACH and Chrome VI-free are EU market entry requirements. OEKO-TEX increasingly required by Amazon Europe. These certs gate Printify's access to institutional and EU buyer segments. EU market, B2B buyers, retailers, brands sourcing through Printify. Required for any decorator targeting the corporate gifting segment. LWG Gold unlocks B2B contracts that individual SKU pricing can't capture — higher volume, higher margin per deal
eco_claims vegetable-tanned · recycled · carbon-offset · Rainforest Alliance · traceable hide Sustainable leather is the fastest-growing segment — Etsy searches for "sustainable leather" grew 45% YoY. "Traceable hide" (farm-to-product) is becoming a buyer requirement in ethical fashion. Carbon-offset leather commands a measurable premium in B2C. This field unlocks the highest willingness-to-pay buyer segment. Ethical fashion buyers, Gen Z premium segment, sustainability-led brands. Lowest price sensitivity, highest loyalty. Eco-labelled SKUs: 15–25% ASP premium + lower churn rate vs non-labelled equivalent
