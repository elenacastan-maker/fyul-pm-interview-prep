Thank you for the task. I found it genuinely compelling — the leather taxonomy gap is one of those problems where the fix is well-defined but the path to it needs careful sequencing to de-risk a meaningful investment.

Below I'm sharing a brief summary of how I approached the work, the data proxies I relied on, and the key decisions I made along the way. The full PRD is linked at the end.

1 · How I framed the problem

My starting point was not "how do we add leather?" but "why are Printify decorators routing their best orders elsewhere?" The answer is structural: the platform's catalog schema doesn't have the fields required to list genuine leather properly — no grade, no tanning method, no origin. Without those fields, premium buyers searching on Etsy and Amazon can't find Printify sellers. The platform is invisible to the most valuable segment of its own market.

From there the hypothesis became testable: if we fix the taxonomy and add FTC-compliant auto-classification, decorators consolidate their leather spend on Printify. The revenue question becomes: at what order velocity does the $340K investment pay back?

3 · The lean PM process — why I structured it as a phase gate

The $340K can't be spent upfront when the demand signal is unvalidated. My approach was to design a kill gate at every stage where the investment commitment increases — each phase must earn the right to spend the next:

PhaseSpendWhat we learnKill condition
PoC$0Do existing decorators want this?<5 signed LOIs → stop, zero engineering spent
Design Sprint$20KCan decorators self-onboard without Ops?Prototype usability <60% → redesign before building
MVP 1$80KCan the AI classify leather reliably?MAT-001 auto-approve <88% → do not launch
MVP 2$120KDoes real demand exist on Shopify?<4 orders/SKU/week → pause, redesign funnel
MVP 3 + Post-launch$120KDo all 3 channels scale at base case?<15 orders/SKU at Month 2 → KILL, reallocate team

The hardest kill gate is at Month 2 post-launch — 8 weeks after MVP 3, when Amazon and Etsy indexing noise has cleared and we have a clean, comparable 3-channel read. Below 15 orders/SKU at that point means the investment doesn't recover even at 36 months. There's no ambiguity in the decision.

4 · Key trade-offs I made explicitly

  • Shopify pilot before Amazon/Etsy: Shopify has no SP-API approval dependency and no 30-day indexing lag. It's Printify's primary channel (60%+ of GMV). Starting there gives us a faster, cleaner demand signal before committing the Amazon integration budget.
  • 8 schema fields, not 15+: I scoped the MVP1 taxonomy to the 8 fields that directly affect pricing, compliance, and search ranking. More fields = more complexity, longer onboarding, higher form error rate. The 8 I chose capture 90%+ of the pricing differentiation available in the leather market.
  • Static mockup engine, not dynamic: A dynamic render engine is a 12–16 week build that sits on the critical path. Static pre-rendered textures (3 per quality tier, locked W9 Day 1) protect the timeline. Dynamic rendering is deferred to v2 once the business case is validated.
  • 5 decorators, not 20: The goal is a clean velocity signal (orders/SKU), not statistical significance at cohort level. 5 decorators × 30 SKUs = 150 active listings — enough for Etsy/Amazon category algorithms to index the supplier and produce organic traffic data.
