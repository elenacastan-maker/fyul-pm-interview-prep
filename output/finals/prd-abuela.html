The Leather PRD

Explained for anyone — no tech background needed
The one sentence version: Printify is missing the right labels for real leather products, which means sellers lose their best customers to competitors. This plan fixes that — in stages, spending money only when the data says it's working.

Part 1 — What problem are we solving?

Imagine explaining it to your grandma over coffee ☕
👵
Cariña, what is Printify exactly? Is it like a shop?
🙋
Think of it like a vending machine for personalised gifts. A seller designs a product — say, a leather wallet with someone's name on it. The customer pays online. Printify sends the design to a factory, the factory makes it, and ships it directly to the customer. The seller never touches it.
👵
That sounds clever! So what's the problem?
🙋
The vending machine doesn't know the difference between real leather and fake leather. It has no label for "this is full-grain Italian leather" vs "this is plastic pretending to be leather". So when a customer searches for a premium leather wallet, Printify's sellers are invisible — the website can't show them because it doesn't know what they're selling.
👵
And the sellers go elsewhere?
🙋
Exactly. They go to a competitor called PrintKK. But PrintKK forces them to buy at least 50 units at once — no flexibility, no custom orders. Printify could offer the same quality with just one unit at a time. We're losing the most profitable orders on the whole platform.

Part 2 — What's the fix?

👵
So you just need to add some labels to the website?
🙋
That's the heart of it! We're adding 8 new quality labels — like "full-grain leather", "Italian origin", "vegetable-tanned", "eco-certified". Once those labels exist, sellers can describe their products properly. Buyers searching for premium leather can find them. And the law says you must label leather correctly — so we also build an AI system that checks every listing automatically.
👵
8 labels and an AI checker. That sounds expensive!
🙋
It's €340,000 in total — but we don't spend it all at once. We test at every step. If the test fails, we stop and we've only spent a little. It's like asking your neighbour if they want to buy your homemade jam before opening a jam factory.
👵
Smart! And what does each label actually mean for the seller?
🙋
Real examples from the market:
• "Full-grain leather" label → 40–60% higher price than the same product in fake leather
• "Italian origin" label → 20–35% more per product
• "Eco-certified" label → 15–25% more + customers who come back more often
Each label is like a quality stamp that buyers trust and pay more for.

👵
But do people actually buy leather gifts online? How do you know there's a market?
🙋

• On Amazon USA, one leather wallet product sells 8,365 units per month. Just one product. That's real demand.

The demand exists. Printify just can't capture it today because the labels are missing.

Part 4 — How do we do it without risking everything?

👵
What if nobody buys? You lose all that money?
🙋
That's exactly what the plan protects against. We do it in 4 steps. We only go to the next step if the current one works:
1
Step 0 — Ask first (costs $0): Before writing one line of code, we call 50 existing Printify sellers and ask "would you use this?" We need 5 to say yes and sign a letter. If fewer than 5 say yes → we stop. We've spent nothing.
2
Step 1 — Design the door ($20K): We design the entry form that sellers use to list their leather products. We test it with real people. If they can't figure it out → we redesign before spending more.
3
Step 2 — Build the labels + AI checker ($80K): We add the 8 quality labels to Printify's system, and build the AI that checks every listing is legally correct. It needs to approve 88% automatically — if it can't, the system isn't ready.
4
Step 3 — Open a test shop on Shopify ($120K): We let 5 sellers list their leather products on Shopify first (no Amazon/Etsy yet — those take longer to start showing results). If we get at least 4 sales per product per week → demand is real. If not → we redesign before opening on Amazon and Etsy.
5
Step 4 — Open everywhere ($120K): Add Amazon and Etsy. Wait 2 months for honest results. ≥15 sales/product/month → KEEP GOING ✓ Under 15 → STOP, cut losses
👵
So you can stop before losing all the money?
🙋
Exactly. The hardest stop point is after Step 3 — at that point we've spent $220K, not $340K. And the very first step costs zero. We only spend big money once we have evidence the idea works. That's lean product management — validate before you build.
The magic question at Month 2
15 orders/product/month
Above this → expand to 1,500 products, 10 sellers  |  Below this → stop, save the remaining $120K
