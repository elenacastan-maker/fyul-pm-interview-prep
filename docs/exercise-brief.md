Expanding into premium leather goods
The Setting
You are a Product Manager in the Supply pillar of Printify. Among other topics the Supply pillar is
responsible for the onboarding of new products and decoration methods (for example direct to film
printing, embroidery etc.) to Printify. You are responsible for one of 5 engineering teams in the Supply
Pillar.
The users of your systems include Decorators as well as internal operational and commercial teams. To
successfully bring new products live, internal teams and Decorators are both required to act.
The Situation
Your colleagues from Category Management are excited as a set of Decorators are purchasing premium
leather goods which can be decorated. The Category Managers believe merchants are keen on creating
designs on these products and consumers are excited about purchasing them.
The Task
Your task is to convince Product Leadership to agree with you. Either in that you have deemed to
prioritise the initiative or to not prioritise.
The introduction of premium leather goods would require changes to backend and frontend systems.
Additionally operational resources are required for any manual steps as well as commercial teams for
ensuring a successful go to market. You may choose to prioritize or not prioritise the initiative.
Please gather any market information you find useful and feel free to estimate any information which you
do not have access to but believe you would have access to as a Product Manager inside the
organisation.
At Printify we create agreements within Product Leadership through written documents. For this example
please create a maximum two page written document. You may use any format you are familiar with
(Product Requirements Doc, Amazon PRFAQ, etc) to build consensus in the leadership.
